## Why This Matters

Artificial intelligence (AI) is rapidly changing the way people learn, work, create, communicate, and make decisions.

New tools promise greater efficiency and access to information on an unprecedented scale. Organizations are racing to adopt AI. Students are using it while learning. Professionals are using it to automate portions of their work. Developers are using it to generate code. These technologies are becoming woven into everyday life.

Yet the capabilities of AI alone cannot answer deeper questions.

Should every task be automated? How do we remain committed to truth when AI can often generate convincing, error-ridden responses? What happens to learning when thinking is increasingly delegated to machines? How do we ensure technology does not weaken our relationships with others?

Faithful Automation is a framework for discerning whether and how automation should be used within human vocations. Faithful Automation supports humans in answering these kinds of questions.

# AI is an Ocean

The ocean is vast, powerful, beautiful, and also dangerous.

For centuries, it has invited exploration, trade, discovery, and connection. Yet sailors cannot survive by embracing the sea. They navigate it with maps, compasses, experience, judgment, and care for those entrusted to them.

Artificial intelligence presents a similar challenge.

AI offers remarkable possibilities. It can amplify human capabilities, accelerate learning, reduce burdensome work, and unlock new forms of creativity. At the same time, it can distort truth, weaken understanding, encourage unhealthy dependence, enable dishonesty, and reshape relationships in ways that are not immediately understood.

How do we navigate AI faithfully in our vocations? By rejecting it, we may neglect a useful tool to serve our neighbors. By embracing it, we may become lost at sea.

Faithful Automation seeks to provide a framework for this navigation.

Just as sailors must know their destination, trust their compass, maintain their vessel, and care for their crew, those using AI must cultivate habits of discernment that guide the faithful use of powerful technologies.

Technology will change.

Our human responsibilities to love and serve our neighbors will not.

## The Framework

Faithful Automation is organized around four areas of discernment. Each one helps us ask ourselves different kinds of questions before deciding whether and how to use AI or similar technologies.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Examine Intent" icon="seti:search">
    Why am I automating in the first place?

    Technology should not determine our goals. Human beings must first consider the motivations and purposes that drive the adoption of automation. Questions of efficiency are worthwhile, but they are never the whole story.
    
    /framework/examine-intent/
  </Card>

  <Card title="Anchor Truth" icon="seti:config">
    What grounds my understanding of reality?

    AI can assist inquiry, but it is not a source of truth. Large language models generate outputs by predicting likely patterns rather than determining what is ultimately true. Human beings require reliable foundations for evaluating information, testing claims, and understanding reality. Without a commitment to truth, powerful tools can easily become instruments of confusion.

    /framework/anchor-truth/
  </Card>

  <Card title="Steward Cognition" icon="seti:brain">
    How will this shape human thinking?

    Human thinking deserves careful stewardship. Automation can elevate human capabilities by reducing routine burdens and expanding access to information. At the same time, it can encourage forms of cognitive offloading that weaken understanding, skill development, and intellectual growth.

    /framework/steward-cognition/
  </Card>

  <Card title="Prioritize Relationships" icon="seti:users">
    How will this affect the people entrusted to my care?

    Every decision about automation affects relationships between people: teachers, students, coworkers, customers, family members, neighbors, and communities. Efficient systems may neglect or offload our responsibilities toward others. Faithful use of AI strengthens our ability to love and serve our neighbors without replacing or diminishing those relationships.

    /framework/prioritize-relationships/
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Who Is This For?

Faithful Automation is intended for anyone seeking to navigate AI within their vocation, faithful to God, their neighbors, and themselves.

### Educators

How should AI influence teaching, assessment, and student formation?

### Students

How can AI be used in ways that support genuine learning within a community of learning?

### Software Developers

How can AI-assisted coding improve productivity without eroding understanding or responsibility?

### Church Leaders

How should AI serve humans in ministry and teaching?

### Families

How can technology support flourishing relationships rather than compete with them?

## About the Project

Faithful Automation was developed by Dr. Marcus Gubanyi, Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Concordia University, Nebraska. Faithful Automation emerged from his desire to help students, educators, developers, professionals, and church leaders think more carefully about the role of AI in human vocation.

Many conversations about technology begin with questions of capability:

*What can AI do?*

Faithful Automation begins with different kinds of questions:

*What is the purpose of this task? Why would I automate?*

*What is true? What is trustworthy?*

*How will this impact my cognitive competencies? How am I thinking?*

*Who are the neighbors that I am serving through this automation? How will this affect my relationships?*

By asking these questions first, we can approach powerful technologies as faithful stewards of the vocations entrusted to us.
