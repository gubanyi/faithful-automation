Discernment is best learned through practice. Each case study takes a real or hypothetical scenario and works through it with the [framework](/framework/), asking whether and how AI should be used. Note that the answer is rarely a simple "use AI" or "don't."

Upcoming topics include AI-assisted grading, AI-assisted software development, AI in workplace communication, and AI in research and writing.
