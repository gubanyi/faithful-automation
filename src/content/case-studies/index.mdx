Good judgment is best learned through practice. Each case study examines a real or hypothetical scenario and shows how the [framework](/framework/) can guide decisions about whether and how to use AI. The right answer is rarely a simple "use AI" or "don't."

Upcoming topics include AI-assisted grading, AI-assisted software development, AI in workplace communication, and AI in research and writing.
