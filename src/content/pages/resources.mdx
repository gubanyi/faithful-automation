These sources are cited throughout the site and are good places to start if you want to go deeper. Research on AI moves quickly, so check publication dates and expect new findings to refine these.

## Understanding how AI works

- [Large language models, explained with a minimum of math and jargon](https://www.understandingai.org/p/large-language-models-explained-with) (Timothy B. Lee and Sean Trott). A readable introduction to how LLMs work.
- [Large Language Models explained briefly](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LPZh9BOjkQs) (3Blue1Brown). A short visual introduction.
- [Transformer Explainer](https://poloclub.github.io/transformer-explainer/) (Georgia Tech). Watch a real model predict the next token.
- [What Is ChatGPT Doing … and Why Does It Work?](https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2023/02/what-is-chatgpt-doing-and-why-does-it-work/) (Stephen Wolfram). A longer, deeper explanation.
- [Tracing the thoughts of a large language model](https://www.anthropic.com/research/tracing-thoughts-language-model) (Anthropic). What interpretability research can, and can't yet, see inside a model.
- [The Foundation Model Transparency Index](https://crfm.stanford.edu/fmti/December-2025/index.html) (Stanford CRFM). How much major AI developers disclose about their models.

## How AI tends to behave

- [Why language models hallucinate](https://openai.com/index/why-language-models-hallucinate/) (OpenAI, 2025)
- [Towards understanding sycophancy in language models](https://www.anthropic.com/research/towards-understanding-sycophancy-in-language-models) (Anthropic, 2023)
- [Sycophancy in generative-AI chatbots](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/sycophancy-generative-ai-chatbots/) (Nielsen Norman Group, 2024). A plain-language overview.
- [Sycophantic AI decreases prosocial intentions and promotes dependence](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aec8352) (Cheng et al., *Science*, 2026)
- [AI chatbots remain confident, even when they're wrong](https://www.cmu.edu/news/stories/archives/2025/july/ai-chatbots-remain-confident-even-when-theyre-wrong) (Carnegie Mellon University, 2025)
- [Reasoning models don't always say what they think](https://www.anthropic.com/research/reasoning-models-dont-say-think) (Anthropic, 2025)

## How people use AI

- [What large language models know and what people think they know](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-024-00976-7) (Steyvers et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence*, 2025)
- [The impact of generative AI on critical thinking](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-impact-of-generative-ai-on-critical-thinking-self-reported-reductions-in-cognitive-effort-and-confidence-effects-from-a-survey-of-knowledge-workers/) (Microsoft Research and Carnegie Mellon, 2025)
- [Folk psychological attributions of consciousness to large language models](https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niae013) (Colombatto and Fleming, *Neuroscience of Consciousness*, 2024)
- [How AI and human behaviors shape psychosocial effects of chatbot use](https://www.media.mit.edu/publications/how-ai-and-human-behaviors-shape-psychosocial-effects-of-chatbot-use-a-longitudinal-controlled-study/) (MIT Media Lab and OpenAI, 2025)

## Research by setting

- **Education:** [Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122) (*PNAS*, 2025), [How university students use Claude](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-education-report-how-university-students-use-claude) (Anthropic, 2025), and [Your brain on ChatGPT](https://www.media.mit.edu/publications/your-brain-on-chatgpt/) (MIT Media Lab, 2025)
- **The workplace:** [Navigating the jagged technological frontier](https://www.hbs.edu/faculty/Pages/item.aspx?num=64700) (Harvard Business School, 2023)
- **Health care:** [Automation bias in mammography](https://doi.org/10.1148/radiol.222176) (*Radiology*, 2023)
- **Law:** [Legal models hallucinate in 1 out of 6 (or more) benchmarking queries](https://hai.stanford.edu/news/ai-trial-legal-models-hallucinate-1-out-6-or-more-benchmarking-queries) (Stanford HAI, 2024) and the [AI Hallucination Cases database](https://www.damiencharlotin.com/hallucinations/)
- **News and search:** [AI search has a citation problem](https://www.cjr.org/tow_center/we-compared-eight-ai-search-engines-theyre-all-bad-at-citing-news.php) (*Columbia Journalism Review*, 2025)
- **Teens:** [How and why teens use AI companions](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/research/talk-trust-and-trade-offs-how-and-why-teens-use-ai-companions) (Common Sense Media, 2025)
