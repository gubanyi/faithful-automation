## Why this matters

Artificial intelligence is rapidly changing the way people learn, work, create, communicate, and make decisions.

Students use it while learning. Professionals use it to draft, analyze, and automate parts of their work. Developers use it to generate code. Organizations are racing to adopt it. These tools are quickly becoming woven into everyday life.

Yet what AI *can* do doesn't tell us what we *should* do with it.

Should every task be automated? How do we stay committed to the truth when AI can generate convincing answers that are wrong? What happens to learning when more and more of our thinking is handed to machines? How do we make sure technology strengthens our relationships instead of quietly replacing them?

**Faithful Automation** is a framework for working through questions like these: a practical way to decide *whether* and *how* to use AI, one task at a time.

## AI is an ocean

The ocean is vast, powerful, beautiful, and dangerous.

For centuries it has invited exploration, trade, discovery, and connection. Yet sailors don't survive by surrendering to the sea. They navigate it with charts, instruments, experience, judgment, and care for their crew.

Artificial intelligence presents a similar challenge. It can amplify human abilities, accelerate learning, reduce burdensome work, and unlock new forms of creativity. It can also distort the truth, weaken understanding, encourage unhealthy dependence, enable dishonesty, and reshape relationships in ways we don't immediately notice.

Reject AI entirely, and we may miss a genuinely useful tool. Embrace it without thinking, and we may end up lost at sea.

Sailors must know their destination, check their instruments, maintain their vessel, and look after their crew. People using AI need similar habits of discernment. Faithful Automation offers a framework for building them.

Technology will change. Our responsibility to the truth, to ourselves, and to the people around us will not.

## The framework at a glance

Faithful Automation follows the natural arc of any task: how you **approach** it, how you **engage** with AI along the way, and how you **reflect** afterward.

<CardGrid>
  <Card eyebrow="Before" title="Approach" href="/framework/#approach">

  1. What task am I completing, and what is its purpose?
  2. What is my intent in using AI for this task?

  </Card>
  <Card eyebrow="During" title="Engage" href="/framework/#engage">

  3. How am I anchoring truth, knowing AI is neither transparent nor fully trustworthy?
  4. How am I stewarding my own thinking, knowing that tasks shape who I become?

  </Card>
  <Card eyebrow="After" title="Reflect" href="/framework/#reflect">

  5. Did AI replace human connections that strengthen my relationships?
  6. How did AI affect the joy I find in my work?

  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Who is this for?

Faithful Automation is for anyone who wants to use AI thoughtfully, including:

- **Educators** deciding how AI should shape teaching, assessment, and student growth.
- **Students** who want AI to support genuine learning rather than replace it.
- **Software developers** looking for productivity without losing understanding of, or responsibility for, their code.
- **Professionals and leaders** bringing AI into their teams and organizations without losing sight of the people they serve.
- **Parents and families** who want technology to support healthy relationships rather than compete with them.
