The [framework](/framework/) works one task at a time. Perspectives step back to the bigger questions (e.g., what happens to human skill as AI grows more capable, who should control AI, how education should respond).

Each perspective is the personal view of its Christian author, and each one is signed. So far, all of them are written by Marcus Gubanyi. Read them as one Christian working through hard questions, and bring your own discernment.
