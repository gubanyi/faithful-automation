# Faithful Automation

Faithful Automation is a framework for discerning whether and how artificial intelligence and other automating technologies should be used within a particular vocation, organization, or context.

Rather than assuming that automation is inherently good or inherently bad, the framework encourages careful reflection on the use of AI based on purpose, truth, cognition and relationships. Its goal is to help individuals and organizations make wise decisions to apply these technologies faithfully.

## Purpose

Artificial intelligence is changing how we learn, work, create, communicate, and serve others. New tools appear regularly, often promising greater efficiency, productivity, or convenience. Yet not every task should be automated, and not every use of AI serves the people affected by it.

Faithful Automation provides a structured approach for evaluating technology through the lens of vocation and stewardship. It seeks to answer questions such as:

- Should this process be automated?
- What is gained by automation?
- What might be lost?
- How does this technology affect the people involved?
- Does this use of AI support or undermine the purpose of the activity?
- What responsibilities remain with human beings?

## Core Convictions

The Faithful Automation framework is built on several convictions:

- Technology is a tool, not an authority.
- Efficiency is valuable but not the highest good.
- Human beings are more than producers of outputs.
- Learning, formation, and service often matter as much as the final product.
- Decisions about automation should consider the impact on people, relationships, and communities.
- Discernment requires both wisdom and context.
